Drag from a dot to lay a pipe to the other dot of the same color. Pipes can't cross, and every square has to be filled before the level counts.

Drag over another pipe to cut it. Drag back along your own pipe to shorten it, or tap a dot to clear its pipe. Solve a level using one move per color and no hints for a perfect.

Keys: arrows move around the board, Enter picks up or drops a pipe, Z undo, R restart, H hint, N next level.
